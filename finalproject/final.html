The Marathon Guidebook

This name was chosen because the focus of the site is providing a guide through information on certain marathons

Site Purpose - The purpose of this site is to guide users through the sea of information about the Abbott World Marathons Majors, and also other majorly sought-after or once-in-a-lifetime experience marathons so that it's easy to find the needed registration information and other information before signing up.
